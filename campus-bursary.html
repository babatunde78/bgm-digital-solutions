Know the boundaries

Designed for controlled spreadsheet work.

  • Designed for one authorised writer at a time; workbook views do not provide secure role-based access.
  • Excel controls rely on staff procedures and are not tamper-proof security controls.
  • Payroll features check imported information and compare periods; they are not a statutory payroll engine.
  • Bank matching requires human review. The workbook does not connect to a bank or automatically approve matches.
  • Full Suite ledger and statement schedules need institution-approved accounting mappings and policies.
  • Microsoft 365 Excel for Windows, 64-bit is the target; other platforms and versions require separate validation.
