# Market and positioning research

Research checked 17 November 2026. Public product pages establish stated capabilities and advertised prices; they do not establish every buyer's use case or willingness to pay. The product opportunity below is a hypothesis for customer interviews, not a proven competitor gap.

## Narrow workflow opportunity (hypothesis)

Target an accountant/bookkeeper who receives a sales register and separate bank or settlement `.xlsx` export, often with different headers and inconsistent reference/date formatting. The proposed workflow is: map the two exports, normalize common formats without changing original values, see why a candidate matched, keep many-to-many/repeated-reference cases in a confirmation queue, preserve source rows and export a review log.

The evidence supports that available tools already offer spreadsheet comparisons, deduplication, Power Query merges, and reconciliation software with potential-match review. It does **not** prove that those products fail this exact workflow, that accountants in Nigeria need another desktop tool, or that local processing is an important buying reason. The hypothesized advantage is the combination of local file handling, explicit match explanations, conservative ambiguous treatment and source-preserving report in a small standalone utility. Validate with Nigerian accountants using scrubbed representative exports and paid trials before making stronger claims.

## Suggested current price

Recommended one-time launch price: **₦15,000 (about US$11.07)**. This is a proposed product price, not a measured Nigerian market price. The amount is within the observed US$9.99–$18.67 range of the two public spreadsheet listings cited above; the products and marketplaces are not directly equivalent. A Nigerian reference page reported CBN central reference ₦1,355.30/USD on 17 November 2026, so ₦15,000 ÷ ₦1,355.30 ≈ US$11.07. This USD figure is display-only and will vary with FX rates and card conversion.

Rate source: [Canjean CBN central reference](https://canjean.com/official-rate/central-bank-of-nigeria), checked 17 November 2026. The site displayed NGN 1,355.30/USD for that date and states the CBN updates its rate on business days. This remains an indicative third-party display, not a same-day independently downloaded CBN row; the USD amount is not a checkout currency.
