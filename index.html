How do I get my files?

After Paystack checkout, return to the confirmation page to download. Keep that page in the same browser. For access on another device, contact BGM with your payment reference. Automatic access emails are currently unavailable.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. Request a refund within 7 days of purchase. Contact BGM with your product name, checkout email and payment reference. Read the refund policy.

Can a tool be customised?

Ask before buying if you need a different workflow, output or setup. Discuss a custom solution.

Do the guides guarantee a result?

No. They provide guidance and preparation resources. They do not guarantee a job, scholarship, client or income.
