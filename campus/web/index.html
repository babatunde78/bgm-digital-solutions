Settlement accounting and explicit differences

These controls transfer previously recorded clearing amounts into the configured bank account, with explicit fees and reviewed variance. Income accounts are not eligible. Nothing executes a bank payment.

Propose an account profile

Choose mapped accounts from an approved policy. The tolerance limit is an explicit institution decision, never an automatic write-off.

Propose a specific difference

Bank-side amounts explain unmatched bank gross; source-side amounts explain a matched source's remaining book amount. Every amount and reason needs separate approval within both profile limits.

Prepare a settlement preview

Complete reviewed gross matching and approved differences are required. Both source clearing entries must exist; directly booked bank cash cannot be counted again. Preparation freezes its matching/difference links until cancellation or coordinated backout.
