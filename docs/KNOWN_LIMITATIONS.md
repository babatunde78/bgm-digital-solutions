# Known limitations

## Budget continuation

Initial expense budgets, commitments, journal-backed actual attribution and independently approved releases now work in the local Connected pilot; earlier blanket statements that every budget control is missing are superseded only for this slice. Revisions, virements, capital budgets, requisitions/POs/receiving, supplier bills, payment schedules, staff advances and complete expenditure dashboards remain pending. Restricted-fund classification does not enforce grant conditions. Reports cover configured expense accounts, not all spending, and reserve future approved obligations conservatively. Manual journals can precede attribution; exceptions block new commitments and close rather than imposing universal pre-spend control. One account/period/fund combination has one basis. Automatic rollover, pagination/capacity benchmarks, timed stale banners, simultaneous sessions, native/mobile rendering, hosted identity and managed restoration remain pending; no production release is claimed.

## Settlement continuation

Explicit, independently approved bank/book residuals and account profiles now support dual-basis settlement/fee/variance journals, source-clearing checks, link freezing and exact coordinated backout. Earlier statements that all settlement/fee/tolerance controls are missing are superseded for this transfer mode only. No thresholds or recognition rules are supplied as customer defaults. Direct-to-bank receipts, deferred-until-settlement recognition policies and unsupported account treatments are not silently converted; duplicate transfers fail. Profile editing/revocation, pending-void rejection, partial backout, multi-currency conversion, complete reporting/export and broad production/security/accounting acceptance remain pending. All provider verification/payment execution, native/mobile, actual concurrent sessions and managed restoration remain unverified or unimplemented.

## Treasury continuation

Generic CSV mapping/staging, row-level errors/control totals, independent import acceptance, duplicate protection, exact/amount/partial suggestions, reviewed split/grouped matching and independently approved match voids now exist locally. This is not a working bank/provider API integration or payment execution. Matching creates no ledger entries or new income; opening balances, clearing-to-bank/fee journal integration, approved nonzero tolerances, earlier-bank timing treatment, accepted-import replacement/voiding, complete pagination/export and original-file secure storage remain pending. No screenshot proves settlement. Request caps are not benchmarks; native/mobile, real provider verification, simultaneous connections and managed recovery remain unverified. See CONNECTED_TREASURY.md for precise semantics.

## Fee scheduling/batch continuation

Session/programme/level setup, per-session enrolment, versioned fee proposals/approval, row-level billing preview and independently approved transactional batch draft generation now exist locally. The guard covers controlled batches using stable institution/student/session/fee keys, not all ad-hoc invoices or duplicate real identities. Generated invoices still need separate accounting approval/posting; no batch payment execution occurs. Rebilling/replacement after cancellation or correction, enrolment edits/multiple programmes, scholarships/discounts/penalties, bulk imports, complete pagination, campus/study-mode filters and native/concurrent/hosted capacity acceptance remain pending. The 100-student request limit is not a benchmark. See CONNECTED_BILLING.md; prior missing-feature statements are superseded only for this pilot scope.

## Coordinated correction continuation

Credits, recorded refunds/chargebacks, allocation releases and full invoice/receipt reversals now have local SQL/API/forms and source-accounting checks. These supersede earlier statements that all source corrections are missing. No bank/provider money is sent or externally verified. Full reversal is limited to an original without remaining allocations or prior reductions; reversal of a posted correction itself, whole-chain/cascading batches and combined release/refund execution remain pending. All-date source reports now expose gross/adjustment/net totals under schema version 2. Native browser/mobile, true simultaneous connections, hosted identity/API controls, bank reconciliation and managed recovery remain pending; there is no production launch claim.

## Revenue continuation — local pilot

Student master, line-based invoice drafts, receipt recording, partial allocations, unapplied/excess receipts, source approvals, atomic accounting posting and both manual/approved-rule entry modes now exist locally. Rules require institution-authored treatment and independent approval; full-amount two-leg entries/non-recognition are the only automatic recipes supported. Complex splits use manual instructions. This does not certify policy compliance or reconcile every possible subledger automatically.

Source credit/refund/reversal/chargeback/release workflows are missing; standalone source-journal corrections are intentionally blocked. No live financial use is accepted. Programme/level fee schedules, batch billing, scholarships, bank settlement/reconciliation, ageing, complete pagination/export, period/session filters, rule editing/revocation and document release remain pending. Totals cover all posted source dates/sessions, not a financial-year dashboard. Native browser/mobile, true concurrent-session tests, provider verification, hosted recovery and production deployment remain pending. CONNECTED_REVENUE.md supersedes earlier missing-feature statements only for the listed implemented flows.

## Accounting configuration continuation

Accounts, non-overlapping financial periods, cash/accrual policy proposals, independent approval, mapped-account enforcement, configuration schedules and period locks now have local database/API/form implementations. Earlier missing-feature statements are superseded for those functions only. Policy narratives and named mappings do not automate recognition rules or validate statutory statements. Editing/rejection/withdrawal, legacy mapping migration, account deactivation, period reopening, complete close/rollover and source-module accounting remain pending. Existing legacy policies without mappings cannot create new journals after migration 003; legacy reversals may therefore need a reviewed mapping migration. There is no silent data conversion. Multi-connection race tests, live-provider/native browser and hosted recovery checks remain open.

Local sign-in/logout adapters, HTTP routes, institution provisioning, staff-role assignment, journal schedules/transitions and linked reversal drafts now exist. ## Connected foundation update — 8 November 2026

The isolated ledger migration and verified-user gateway library now exist; earlier statements that all connected controls are missing are superseded only for this tested slice. Neither is deployed. Local PostgreSQL tests simulate a trusted Auth subject; gateway tests mock HTTP boundaries. Login/MFA/session management, routes, provisioning, membership/policy/configuration administration, currency configuration, reversals/edit/reapproval, multi-connection concurrency, operational subledgers, reports, document/signature storage, imports/exports and backup restoration remain pending. Period flags currently require a privileged operator. Synthetic policy fixtures are not approved customer policies. No managed backup, production isolation or complete Connected Suite claim is made.

Stage 2 provides a single-writer Excel pilot generated from shared schemas with operational schedules, formulas and manual journal/control reports. See EXCEL_IMPLEMENTATION.md and the native verification report for precise implementation and evidence. The Excel pilot packages are now available through the storefront described below; their institution-specific accounting mappings and opening balances still require review. No production Connected tenant application, persisted quote workflow, or Connected Suite is available.

There is no tenant migration, authentication/permission enforcement, secure audit/storage/signature release or concurrency control. Excel period locks/approvals require staff procedures; local billing/reversal helpers check lock metadata but cannot prevent workbook edits. There is no automatic journal posting, close/rollover, in-place upgrade, authenticated signature or statutory financial statement. The price book is versioned with an effective date, while Supabase publication and immutable customer orders remain separate. In-memory quote snapshots are not persisted invoices.

No Mac/web/other-build compatibility, general commercial capacity, Campus browser/provider acceptance or deployment is claimed. A 100-row synthetic workload now passes native checks on Excel 16.0 build 13127; larger datasets, real customer shapes, payroll/assets/grants and per-transaction journal volume remain unbenchmarked. The generator prepares 100 rows per source table and rejects larger datasets. Native local CSV Power Query refresh and missing-file failure handling passed eight checks on that build. Other sources and platforms are unverified. Pivot reports require recalculation and explicit refresh. Wide source tables may span two pages across and multiple pages down; filter blank IDs before printing. Full-suite control comparison requires an unfiltered academic-session scope.

## Excel storefront release boundary — 8 November 2026

Package A, Package B and the Full Excel Suite are published on the BGM storefront. Their release ZIPs were uploaded to private, immutable Blob paths and read-back verified during the production build; exact catalogue rows were published and the live catalogue, product routes and NGN prices were checked. The ZIPs are not part of the public asset build. The owner confirmed a real production purchase and download on 8 November 2026; this is owner-reported, not independently matched to provider or database records in this session. Automated tests use synthetic payment/provider responses. Direct anonymous Blob denial and cross-browser visual inspection remain unverified.

Checkout is one-time NGN; automatic annual renewal is not implemented. Standard setup and training require manual onboarding after purchase. The Excel products remain single-writer workbooks with procedure-based controls; institutional accounting mappings, opening balances and professional acceptance must be reviewed before financial use. Connected modules remain unavailable for purchase. See `IMPLEMENTATION_STATUS.md` for deployment evidence and URLs.

The dashboard timestamp remains a generated-snapshot cue, not evidence that all sources refreshed. The optional import preview now includes its own successful-refresh UTC timestamp; a failed refresh can retain old preview data and timestamp. Excel's clock follows the workstation, so review timezones and source completeness. General ledger control checks cover overall balance, receivables, supplier payables and receipts clearing; they do not prove every grant/asset/advance schedule has been posted. Asset disposal cutoff/gain-or-loss calculations are manual. One 100-row, nine-column supplier-bill printout was verified across eight pages; other wide tables and real institution text/layouts still require acceptance.

Institution name and product name are linked to key outputs; Config colour is metadata and does not automatically recolour cells. Logo insertion/replacement is manual, with no authenticated upload or type/size validation. Asset disposals, fee setup, grants, scholarships, officer approval evidence and cashier handovers involve manual schedules/review. Support hours, accounting basis/mappings, statutory/local legal research, competitor comparison, hosting and legal terms still require decisions/evidence before launch. No compliance claims are made.
