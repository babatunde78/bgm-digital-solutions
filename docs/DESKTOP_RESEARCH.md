# Document automation research

Accessed 9 November 2026. Official documentation only; this is a scoped document review, not independent hands-on acceptance or a complete market survey. Prices are public page observations, not BGM prices, quotations or guaranteed local availability. Taxes, plan period and region may differ.

| Product / official source | Documented capability | Public price observation | Dependencies and evidence limits |

Offline institutional deployment and redistribution rights are not verified in reviewed pages. Do not infer absence of other features. |

Its reviewed tools are general PDF workflows. Institutional accounting/document policy coverage not verified. |

“Documented” in the table means vendor documentation supports that capability. No independent feature absence or failure was established. Where a capability is **not verified**, it must not be marketed as a competitor limitation. Hosted API dependencies are documented workflow dependencies, not evidence of every possible deployment limitation.

BGM's proposed opportunity is a bursary-focused, offline workflow with curated templates, validated source imports, branded output, transparent exceptions, retained revisions and retrieval/recovery. Whether institutions value these enough to buy requires customer interviews and an authorised pilot; worldwide uniqueness is not claimed. Do not call this a complete accounting system.
