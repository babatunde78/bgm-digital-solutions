Full Excel annual discount is 20% against A+B. The storefront accepts a one-time payment for the first-year total; automatic renewals are not implemented. The customer receives a 12-month licence, updates and standard support; renewal requires a separate agreed payment and does not happen automatically. Monthly displays, if added, must say “equivalent; billed annually”. Future independently approved USD price books are separate from display conversion. The historical reference rate is NGN 1,348.11/USD, owner-supplied 7 November 2026; it is not live or independently verified. `usdEquivalent` calculates from original NGN amounts and rounds the combined total only once. Exchange changes never alter existing contracts.

Connected provisional base is one campus/ten named staff; final quote depends on measured capacity, hosting/storage, migration, integrations and support. Setup includes logo/branding, standard academic/account configuration, one agreed clean-data import, opening reconciliation and two remote training sessions. Taxes, Microsoft licences, provider fees, travel, historical cleaning and custom development are separate; no automatic unverified tax.

Standard setup includes logo/branding configuration, standard academic/account setup, one agreed clean-data import, opening-balance reconciliation and two remote training sessions. Standard support covers questions about using the delivered workbooks through the existing BGM support channels; it excludes accounting/legal advice, custom development, historical data cleaning and 24/7 service. No fixed response-time promise is made. The existing storefront refund terms apply; see its published support/refund page. Excel files are editable and offline copy protection is limited; expiry does not remove customer data or block historical exports. Price publication/effective dates are versioned here; existing orders keep their stored first-year price.
